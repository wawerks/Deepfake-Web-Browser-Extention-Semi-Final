Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

This Extension analyzes images for authenticity using Sightengine AI Detection API. We do not collect personal information such as names, emails, or browsing history.

What is transmitted:

  • Image data (URLs or file bytes) sent to Sightengine for analysis
  • Detection results and confidence scores

Important:

  • Images are processed only on demand, never automatically
  • Images are not stored, logged, or retained after analysis
  • Your images are not used to train AI systems

Third-Party Service: Sightengine (https://sightengine.com/) performs the analysis and applies its own privacy policies.
